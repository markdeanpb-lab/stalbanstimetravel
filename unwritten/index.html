Abilities

Pass through — walk through thin barriers marked with a dashed teal edge: railings, plank partitions, doors.
Inspect — read evidence, even out of reach: inside drawers, behind glass, in someone's pocket. E / click.
Possess — enter a marked object and make it do the one thing it does: ring, switch, release. Once per object per run.
Whisper — a person turns and comes to look at where you are. It doesn't control them. Once per person per run. Q
Enter a grave (not an ability) — walk into the last minutes of the person buried there. Costs one journey.
